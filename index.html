Our History

OUR JOURNEY THROUGH THE YEARS

From a humble beginning in 1936 in Nakuru, Car & General is now the company behind a wide range of power generation, automotive, construction and industrial engineering products in East Africa. Today, our product range represents several international brand names.

Established as Car & General Equipment Limited in Nakuru before relocating to Nairobi in 1937, with initial objectives to import and distribute automotive parts and white goods.
1936
Introduced tyre-retreading, and was actually one of the pioneers of local industry.
1941
One of the companies to be quoted on the Nairobi Securities Exchange.
1950
Branch offices in Kampala and Dar-es-Salaam formed into separate subsidiaries Car & General (Uganda) Limited and Car & General (Tanzania) Limited.
1960's
Dramatic change in revenue base that saw de-investment from original businesses.
1996
Moved to new premises along Lusaka Road.
1997
2020
Completed the Nairobi Mega C&G's real estate venture, along Mombasa Road.
2018
Signed an agreement with NIIT of India to operate NIIT C&G Limited, an IT and business training facility in Kenya that was launched in 2019.
2017
Signed a joint venture with Cummins to form Cummins C&G Limited.

Acquired a stake in Watu Credit Limited.
2013
Launched the TVS motorcycle assembly plant in Nakuru.
2005
Became distributor of Cummins in Uganda and Tanzania before becoming spreading to all Eastern African countries in 2006.
2003
Ventured into new product lines. Introduced two-wheelers and three-wheelers as taxis in East Africa, and took over Cummins distributorship in Kenya.
